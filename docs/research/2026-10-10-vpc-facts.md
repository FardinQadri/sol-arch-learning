## Confidence notes

- High confidence: quotas, bandwidth, NACL/SG behaviour, endpoint behaviour, TGW and peering rules (read directly from current user guides).
- Prices: AWS pricing pages are partly dynamic; N. Virginia TGW and VPN numbers come from AWS pricing JSON (awsstatic) and the TGW pricing page example. Treat all as illustrative.
- SG quotas differ if the account raised them; the 1,000 combined cap is documented.
- Several docs now cover newer features (regional NAT, Large Bandwidth VPN, VPN Concentrator, resource endpoints). SAA-C03 questions mostly still target zonal NAT, standard endpoints and classic TGW.
